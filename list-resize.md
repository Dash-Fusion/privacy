# Pixels Only — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-18
**Last updated:** 2026-09-18

## Summary

Pixels Only shows you what a photo says about you — where it was taken, when,
on which phone, and more — and makes copies that carry the picture and nothing
else, smaller if you like. Every copy is read back after it is written, to check
that it carries nothing but the picture.

**Your photos never leave your phone through this app.** They are read and
rewritten on the device. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and there is
no server of ours anywhere. A copy only goes somewhere when you send it there
yourself: into a folder you choose, or to an app you pick from the share sheet.

**The app keeps no history.** There is no list of the photos it has seen, no
count per photo and no record of what any of them said. A tool that removes what
photos say about people should not keep its own record of them.

The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob needs to show ads,
and **no photo, no copy and nothing read out of a photo is any part of that**.

## How photos come in and go out

- **In:** through **Android's own photo picker**, which hands this app only the
  photos you chose, or by **sharing** photos to Pixels Only from another app.
  The app holds **no photo, media or storage permission** on any Android
  version, so it cannot browse your gallery — it sees exactly what you give it.
- **Read on the device:** each photo is read in memory to list what it carries
  (for example GPS coordinates, the date and time, the phone's make and model,
  names, serial numbers, a hidden thumbnail or a motion photo's video). What is
  listed is shown to you and is **not stored**.
- **Out:** copies are saved into **a folder you pick** with Android's own folder
  picker, or handed to **an app you pick** from Android's share sheet. The app
  remembers the folder you chose so it can save there next time; you can make it
  forget the folder in **Settings → Where copies are saved → Forget this folder**.

**Location may already be blanked.** Because the app does not hold Android's
media-location permission, Android may remove a photo's location before handing
it over. When that has happened, the app says so rather than claiming the photo
has no location.

**What a copy cannot hide.** The app removes what the file says, not what the
picture shows. A face, a street sign or the view from a window is still there in
every copy. A copy keeps which way up the picture goes and its colour profile,
because those describe the picture, not you.

## What the app stores on your device

- **App preferences:** your theme and wallpaper-colour choice, the options you
  last used (the kind of copy, the largest side, the compression), the folder
  you chose for copies and its name, when the rewarded ad-free day runs out, and
  a count the app uses to space out ads.
- **The batch in progress:** while you are working, the copies being made sit in
  the app's own private cache, beside a small record of the options used and
  what happened to each picture — so a batch survives Android closing the app in
  the background. That record holds **nothing read out of any photo** — no
  findings, and no names except the copies' own file names. **Closing the batch
  deletes the copies and the record.** Copies you
  saved to your own folder are yours, and stay where you put them.

That is the whole list. There is no database, no name, no email, no location
and no contact anywhere in it. We hold **no copy of any of it**: there is no
analytics SDK in this app, no crash reporter of our own, and no telemetry of
any kind.

The app **opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer**, so
none of this is copied into Google's backup service.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are the only reason the
app needs an internet connection at all; reading photos and making copies work
fully offline.

The app shows **no banner ads, no native ads and no ad on launch**. What it
shows is an occasional full-screen ad after you close a batch whose copies you
saved or shared — never while you pick photos, never while copies are being
made, and never over a folder picker or share sheet. There is also an optional
rewarded ad you can choose to watch, in **Settings → Turn adverts off for a
day**, to switch ads off for 24 hours.

To serve ads, Google receives data from your device, which may include your
device's advertising identifier (`AD_ID`), coarse location inferred from your IP
address, device and app information, and ad-interaction data. **It does not
receive any photo, any copy or anything read out of a photo** — the app never
sends those anywhere, and no ad targeting here is based on them. Google's
processing is done as an independent controller under its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from partner sites and apps:
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

### Consent (UMP)

Where required by law, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform consent
form before ads are initialised, and records your choice. In the EEA and the UK
you are asked about personalised advertising; in covered US states you are
offered the opt-out required there. You can change your decision at any time
from **Settings → Advert privacy options**, and if you decline, every feature of
the app continues to work exactly as before.

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We operate no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We have no
database with your name in it, and no way to identify you or your device. We do
not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet / network state** — solely so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch
  ads. Turning off Wi-Fi and mobile data means no ads load; every photo feature
  still works.
- **Advertising ID (`AD_ID`)** — required by Google Play for apps that serve ads;
  used by AdMob as described above.

That is all of them, and **none is a permission Android stops to ask you
about**. The app requests **no photo or media access, no storage permission, no
media-location permission, no camera, no location, no microphone, no contacts
and no notification permission**. Nothing in it runs when the app is not in
front of you: there are no notifications, no alarms and no background work.

A few more permissions are merged into the final app package by the libraries
the app is built on, so the list Google Play shows you is slightly longer than
the one above. They are the Android Ad Services permissions plus wake-lock and
foreground-service, all from the Google Mobile Ads SDK. None is a permission
Android stops to ask you about, and none is used by any code we wrote.

## Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for use under
the COPPA framework. We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children — as described above, we hold no personal data at all.

## Your choices

- **Ad personalisation:** change your consent decision any time via **Settings →
  Advert privacy options**.
- **Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads** in Android's own
  settings, under **Settings → Google → Ads**.
- **Forget the folder** the app saves copies into, via **Settings → Where copies
  are saved → Forget this folder**.
- **Delete what the app stores** by closing the batch in progress, or by
  clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Because everything lives only on
  your device, that removes it completely and irreversibly — there is no copy
  anywhere else, and no deletion request to send us. Copies you saved into your
  own folders are not touched; they are yours to keep or delete.
