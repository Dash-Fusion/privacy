# List Debt — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-07-04
**Last updated:** 2026-07-04

## Summary

List Debt is an offline-first debt & installment tracker. Your debts are
some of the most personal data you have, and we built the app around that:
everything you enter stays **on your device**. We do **not** create
accounts, do **not** ask for your email, do **not** link to any bank, and
do **not** ship any analytics or tracking SDK. The only data that leaves
your device is what Google AdMob needs to serve ads — and AdMob never sees
your debts.

If that's all you wanted to know, you're done. The sections below spell it
out in detail.

## Who we are

- **App name:** List Debt
- **Developer:** Dash Fusion
- **Support email:** dash.fusion@outlook.com
- **App listing:** Google Play (`com.dashfusion.listdebt`)

## Data the app stores on your device

The following data is created and kept **locally** on your device. It never
leaves the device unless you explicitly export or share it.

- The debts you enter: provider name, category, amounts, installment
  schedules, due dates, paid/unpaid status, and any notes you type
- Documents you attach to a debt (e.g. a contract photo or receipt PDF) —
  copied into the app's **private storage**, where no other app can read them
- App preferences: currency, theme, reminder settings
- If you enable reminders: due-date notifications are scheduled **on the
  device itself** — no server is involved

You can export all of it at any time via **Settings → Back up now** (a
single backup file containing your data and attachments, saved wherever you
choose), and import it again via **Settings → Restore from backup**. The
share/export feature (text, PDF, spreadsheet) likewise generates files
**locally** and hands them to the app you pick — nothing is uploaded by us.

You can wipe everything by uninstalling the app — Android removes the local
database and the private attachment files with it.

We have no servers and no cloud storage. We cannot see, recover, or reset
your data — it is yours alone.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect and process:

- Your device's **Advertising ID** (a resettable identifier that Android
  provides for ads)
- **Coarse location** (typically inferred from IP) and **device/network
  information** that AdMob needs to serve and measure ads
- **Ad interaction events** (impressions, clicks)

AdMob does **not** receive your debts, amounts, providers, attachments, or
preferences. In addition, we block predatory-lending ad categories (payday
/ "fast cash" style ads) in our AdMob configuration — we don't think people
managing debt should be shown more of it. Google's handling of the data
above is governed by Google's own policies:

- [AdMob privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device's
system settings (Android: **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We don't run any backend. We have no analytics SDK, no crash
reporter that phones home, no Firebase, and no custom telemetry. We don't
log app usage anywhere we can read it.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet** — solely so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch ads.
  Disabling Wi-Fi/data simply means no ads load; the app still works fully
  offline.
- **Notifications** (Android 13+, optional) — asked only when you enable
  due-date reminders, so the app can show them. Reminders are scheduled
  locally on your device.

The app does **not** hold standing access to your location, contacts,
microphone, photos, or files. When you attach a document, back up, or
restore, the app uses your device's own file picker and only touches the
files you choose; attachments are stored in the app's private storage.

## Children

List Debt is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed for use
under the COPPA framework. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your choices

- **Export your data** — Settings → Back up now (writes a backup file you
  control, including your attachments)
- **Delete your data** — uninstall the app
