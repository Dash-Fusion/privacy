# Resume Builder — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-11
**Last updated:** 2026-09-11

## Summary

Resume Builder makes a CV on your phone and exports it as a PDF. Everything you
type stays on your phone.

There is no account, no sign-up, no cloud and no server of ours anywhere. We
never see your name, your employment history, your address or the jobs you are
applying for.

This matters more here than in most apps, and it is worth saying why. **A CV is
the most identifying document most people own.** It carries your full name, your
email address, your phone number, where you live, every employer you have had
and when you worked for them. Handing that to a service is a decision, and this
app is built so that you never have to make it: there is nothing to send it to.

The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob needs to show ads,
and none of it is anything you typed.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything is written to this app's **private storage**, which no other app on
your phone can read:

- **Your resumes** — the name you gave each one and when you last edited it
- **Your contact block** — full name, headline, email, phone, location and one
  link, exactly as you typed them
- **Your sections and entries** — job titles, employers, places, start and end
  dates, and your bullet points, in the order you put them in
- **App preferences:** theme, and when the rewarded ad-free window runs out

That is the whole list. There is no photograph anywhere in it, no location fix
and no contact imported from your phone, because the app never asks for any of
them.

We hold **no copy of any of it**. There is no analytics SDK in this app, no
crash reporter of our own, and no telemetry.

The app also **opts out of Android's cloud backup and device-to-device
transfer**, so your CV is never copied into Google's backup service. That is a
deliberate trade and you should know which way it cuts: nothing about your
career is stored off your phone, and **uninstalling the app, or losing the
phone, loses it**. Use **Settings → Save a copy** before you switch phones — it
is the intended way to move your data, and it is never behind an ad.

## The PDF you export

When you export, the app writes the PDF to **the place you choose**, through
Android's own document picker. From that moment the file is an ordinary document
on your phone and this policy no longer governs it — you can send it wherever
you like, and the app has no idea where that is.

The app never uploads it. There is no share link, no hosted copy and no
shortened URL. The **Share** action hands the file to whichever app you pick
using Android's own sheet, and what happens next is between you and that app.

The PDF contains what you typed and nothing else: no tracking pixel, no hidden
metadata about you, no watermark and no advertisement for this app.

## What the app deliberately cannot do

Worth stating plainly, because apps in this category commonly do these things:

- **It has no account and no cloud.** Nothing syncs, nothing is hosted, and
  there is no link anybody could be sent.
- **It does not write your CV for you.** No AI, no suggestions, no phrasing
  help — because generating text would mean sending your employment history to
  somebody's server and having it sit in their logs.
- **It does not import from a PDF or from LinkedIn.** A wrong parse quietly
  damages your own record of your career, and an import would need either a
  server or access to an account of yours.
- **It does not take a photograph or hold one.** No camera permission and no
  photo access. A CV here has no picture on it: it is unlawful to consider one
  in several countries, and most software that reads CVs discards it anyway.
- **It does not read your contacts** and will not offer to fill your details in
  from your phone.
- **It never notifies you.** There is no notification permission and no channel.
- **It does not know where you are.** No location permission of any kind. The
  location on your CV is a line you typed.
- **It does not apply for anything.** Nothing is submitted anywhere. It is your
  own document, for you to send yourself.

## Saving and restoring

You can save a copy of every resume to a file you choose, and restore one back.
**A saved file leaves the app's protection**: it is written where you point it,
in a readable JSON form other apps can open, and it contains everything in the
app — which for this app means your full contact details and your whole
employment history. Keep it somewhere you would keep the CV itself.

Restoring **replaces** what is in the app — it does not merge — and the
confirmation says so before anything is overwritten.

Both the file you save and the file you restore are chosen through Android's own
document picker, which hands the app that one file and grants it nothing else.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are the only reason the
app needs an internet connection at all; writing a CV, reordering it, exporting
the PDF and saving a copy all work fully offline.

The app shows **no banner ads, no native ads and no ads on launch**. The only
moment an occasional full-screen ad can appear is **after a PDF has finished
exporting** — never while you are writing, never on the way into anything, and
never between you and an email you are sending. There is also an optional
rewarded video you can choose to watch to switch ads off for 24 hours. **Nothing
in this app is locked behind it**, and that includes exporting your PDF and
saving a copy of your data.

To serve ads, Google receives data from your device, which may include your
device's advertising identifier (`AD_ID`), coarse location inferred from your IP
address, device and app information, and ad-interaction data. **It does not
receive your name, your contact details, your employment history, any bullet
point you wrote or any PDF you exported.** Google's processing is done as an
independent controller under its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from partner sites and apps:
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

### Consent (UMP)

Where required by law, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform consent
form before ads are initialised, and records your choice. In the EEA and the UK
you are asked about personalised advertising; in covered US states you are
offered the opt-out required there. You can change your decision at any time
from **Settings → Ad privacy options**, and if you decline, every feature of the
app continues to work exactly as before.

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We operate no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We have no
database with your name in it and no way to identify you or your device. We do
not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

## About what the app does with your dates

A role here has a start and, if it has finished, an end. The app sorts your
roles by those dates, works out how long each one lasted, and tells you when an
end date falls before its start. That is arithmetic on figures you typed.

It is not a check of anything: the app does not know whether an employer exists,
whether the dates are right, or whether anybody will agree with them.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet / network state** — solely so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch
  ads.

That is both of them, and there is no third. The app requests **no location, no
notifications, no camera, no contacts, no photo or media access, no storage
permission, no calendar and no exact-alarm permission**. Exporting a PDF and
saving a copy both work through Android's document picker, which needs no
permission at all.

Seven more permissions are merged into the final app package by the libraries
the app is built on, so the list Google Play shows you is longer than the two
above: the advertising ID (`AD_ID`), the three Android Ad Services permissions,
and wake-lock, foreground-service and job-service, all of which come from the
Google Mobile Ads SDK and its scheduling library. None of them is a permission
Android stops to ask you about, and none is used by any code we wrote.

## Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for use under
the COPPA framework. We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children — as described above, we hold no personal data at all.

## Your choices

- **Ad personalisation:** change your consent decision any time via
  **Settings → Ad privacy options**.
- **Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads** in Android's own
  settings, under **Settings → Google → Ads**.
- **Delete your data** by removing a resume, clearing the app's storage, or
  uninstalling it. Because your CV lives only on your device, that removes it
  completely, with no copy anywhere else and no deletion request to send us.
  A PDF you already exported is an ordinary file on your phone and is not
  removed with the app.
