# Metronome — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-12
**Last updated:** 2026-09-12

## Summary

Metronome keeps time and remembers the songs you practise. Everything it
remembers stays on your phone.

There is no account, no sign-up, no cloud and no server of ours anywhere. Your
setlists are not uploaded, not analysed and not used to train anything. We
cannot see them and we could not hand them to anyone if we were asked, because
we never receive them.

**The app does not use the microphone.** It makes sound; it does not listen.
There is no recording of any kind, and no microphone permission in the app for
one to hide behind.

The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob needs to show ads.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything below is written to this app's **private storage** — a local
database and a preferences file that no other app on your phone can read:

- **Setlists:** the name you gave each one, and when you last edited it
- **Songs:** the name, the tempo in beats per minute, the time signature, which
  beats you accented, how many bars to run for, and their order in the setlist
- **App preferences:** theme, whether to keep the screen on while playing,
  whether to use the wallpaper's colours, when a rewarded ad-free window runs
  out, and a count of finished practice sessions used to decide how often an
  ad may appear

We hold **no copy of any of it**. There is no analytics SDK in this app, no
crash reporter of our own, and no telemetry. None of it is used for
advertising.

The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer
(`allowBackup="false"`), so your setlists are not copied into Google's backup
service either.

**Consequence, stated honestly:** because your setlists exist only on this
phone, uninstalling the app deletes them permanently and we cannot restore
them. **Settings → Save a copy** writes a JSON file wherever you choose, and
that copy is yours to keep.

## Saving a copy is something you do, not something we do

**Save a copy** and **Restore a copy** use Android's own file picker. You
choose where the file goes and where it comes from, the file moves directly
between your phone and that location, and it does not pass through us. We
never see it.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are the only reason the
app needs an internet connection at all; the metronome, the setlists and the
backup all work with no connection whatsoever.

The app shows **no banner ads**, and **never shows an ad while the metronome is
running**. What it shows is an occasional full-screen ad after a practice
session has ended — and only a session that actually ran, not a quick tempo
check — plus an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch to switch ads off
for 24 hours. Nothing in the app is locked behind either one.

To serve ads, Google receives data from your device, which may include your
device's advertising identifier (`AD_ID`), coarse location inferred from your
IP address, device and app information, and ad-interaction data. **It does not
receive your setlists, your songs, your tempos or your preferences** — the app
never sends those anywhere. Google's processing is done as an independent
controller under its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from partner sites and apps:
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

### Consent (UMP)

Where required by law, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform consent
form before ads are initialised, and records your choice. In the EEA and the UK
you are asked about personalised advertising; in covered US states you are
offered the opt-out required there. You can change your decision at any time
from **Settings → Ad privacy options**, and if you decline, every metronome and
setlist feature continues to work exactly as before.

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We operate no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We have no
database with your name in it and no way to identify you or your device. We do
not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

## Permissions the app requests

The whole list, and it is short:

- **Internet / network state (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)** — solely so
  the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch ads. Turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data and
  no ads load; every other feature still works.
- **Advertising ID (`AD_ID`)** — required by Google Play for apps that serve
  ads; used by AdMob as described above.

That is all three of them. The app requests **no microphone**, no storage
permission of any kind, no notifications, no camera, no contacts and no
location. It cannot read your other files.

## Children

This app is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 13 and
over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — as
described above, we hold no personal data at all.

## Your choices

- **Ad personalisation:** change your consent decision any time via
  **Settings → Ad privacy options**.
- **Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads** in Android's own
  settings, under **Settings → Google → Ads**.
- **Delete your data** by deleting setlists in the app, or by clearing the
  app's storage or uninstalling it. Because everything lives only on your
  device, that removes it completely and irreversibly — there is no copy
  anywhere else, and no deletion request to send us.
