# Recipe Keeper — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-03
**Last updated:** 2026-09-03

## Summary

Recipe Keeper is somewhere to keep **your own** recipes. You type them in or
paste them in, and the app organises, searches and scales them.

**Nothing you write ever leaves your phone.** There is no account, no sign-up,
no cloud, and no server of ours anywhere. We cannot see your recipes and we
could not hand them to anyone if we were asked, because we never receive them.

The app also **does not download anything**. It ships with no recipe database,
fetches nothing from the internet, and scrapes no websites. Every recipe in it
is one you put there.

The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob needs to show ads,
and **nothing about your recipes is part of that**.

## What the app stores, and where

All of the following is created and kept **locally**, in a database only this
app can read, and never leaves your device unless you export or share it
yourself:

- **Recipes:** the title, your note, servings, prep and cook time
- **Ingredients:** the quantity, unit and item text you typed, and whether an
  ingredient is marked as one that does not scale
- **Steps:** the text you typed, and which ones you have ticked off while
  cooking
- **Tags:** whatever words you chose
- **App preferences:** units, theme, and when the rewarded ad-free window runs
  out

We hold **no copy of any of it**. There is no analytics SDK in this app, no
crash reporter of our own, and no telemetry. Nothing you type is transmitted
anywhere, and none of it is used for advertising.

The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your
recipes are not copied into Google's backup service either.

**Consequence, stated honestly:** because the data exists only on your phone, we
cannot recover it if you lose the device. Use **Settings → Export a backup** and
keep the file somewhere safe. It is one readable JSON file, so any other program
can open it.

## Sharing a recipe is something you do, not something we do

The app can share a single recipe as plain text into whatever app you choose —
a message, an email, a note. That happens only when you tap share, the
destination is chosen by you in Android's own share sheet, and the text goes
directly from your phone to that app. It does not pass through us.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are the only reason the
app needs an internet connection at all; typing, searching, scaling and cooking
all work fully offline.

The app shows **no banner ads**, and **never shows an ad in cook mode** — not on
a step list, not while the screen is being held awake for you. What it shows is
an occasional full-screen ad in the browsing parts of the app, and an optional
rewarded ad you can choose to watch to switch ads off for 24 hours.

To serve ads, Google receives data from your device, which may include your
device's advertising identifier (`AD_ID`), coarse location inferred from your IP
address, device and app information, and ad-interaction data. **It does not
receive your recipes, your ingredients, your notes or your tags** — the app
never sends those anywhere. Google's processing is done as an independent
controller under its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from partner sites and apps:
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

### Consent (UMP)

Where required by law, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform consent
form before ads are initialised, and records your choice. In the EEA and the UK
you are asked about personalised advertising; in covered US states you are
offered the opt-out required there. You can change your decision at any time
from **Settings → Ad privacy options**, and if you decline, every recipe feature
continues to work exactly as before.

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We operate no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We have no
database with your name in it and no way to identify you or your device. We do
not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet / network state** — solely so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch
  ads. Turning off Wi-Fi and mobile data means no ads load; every other feature
  still works.
- **Advertising ID (`AD_ID`)** — required by Google Play for apps that serve
  ads; used by AdMob as described above.

That is all. The app requests **no** camera, no photos or storage, no contacts,
no location, and no microphone. Writing a backup file uses Android's own file
picker, which grants access to the one file you choose and nothing else.

## Children

This app is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 13 and over.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children — as described
above, we hold no personal data at all.

## Your choices

- **Ad personalisation:** change your consent decision any time via
  **Settings → Ad privacy options**.
- **Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads** in Android's own
  settings, under **Settings → Google → Ads**.
- **Export your data** any time via **Settings → Export a backup**.
- **Delete your data** by deleting recipes in the app, or by clearing the app's
  storage or uninstalling it. Because the data lives only on your device, that
  removes it completely and irreversibly — there is no copy anywhere else, and
  no deletion request to send us.
