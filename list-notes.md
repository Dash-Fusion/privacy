# List Notes — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-07-06
**Last updated:** 2026-07-06

## Summary

List Notes is an offline-first notes app. Notes are personal, and we built
the app around that: everything you write stays **on your device**. We do
**not** create accounts, do **not** ask for your email, do **not** sync to
any cloud, and do **not** ship any analytics or tracking SDK. The only data
that leaves your device is what Google AdMob needs to serve ads — and AdMob
never sees your notes.

If that's all you wanted to know, you're done. The sections below spell it
out in detail.

## Who we are

- **App name:** List Notes
- **Developer:** Dash Fusion
- **Support email:** dash.fusion@outlook.com
- **App listing:** Google Play (`com.dashfusion.listnotes`)

## Data the app stores on your device

The following data is created and kept **locally** on your device. It never
leaves the device unless you explicitly export or share it.

- The notes you write: titles, text with formatting, checklists, tables,
  and the optional per-note color
- Files you attach to a note (photos, PDFs, any other file) — **copied**
  into the app's **private storage**, where no other app can read them. The
  app only ever reads these copies for preview; it never modifies your
  original files
- App preferences: theme choice

You can export your notes at any time via **Settings → Back up notes** (a
JSON file containing your notes' content and structure — attachments are
not included in the backup file), and import them again via **Settings →
Restore from backup**. The export features (note → PDF, table →
spreadsheet) likewise generate files **locally** and hand them to the app
you pick via the system share sheet — nothing is uploaded by us.

You can wipe everything by uninstalling the app — Android removes the local
database and the private attachment copies with it.

We have no servers and no cloud storage. We cannot see, recover, or reset
your notes — they are yours alone.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK. It shows an occasional
full-screen ad when you close a note — never while you are writing or
reading. Google may collect and process:

- Your device's **Advertising ID** (a resettable identifier that Android
  provides for ads)
- **Coarse location** (typically inferred from IP) and **device/network
  information** that AdMob needs to serve and measure ads
- **Ad interaction events** (impressions, clicks)

AdMob does **not** receive your notes, attachments, or preferences.
Google's handling of the data above is governed by Google's own policies:

- [AdMob privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device's
system settings (Android: **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We don't run any backend. We have no analytics SDK, no crash
reporter that phones home, no Firebase, and no custom telemetry. We don't
log app usage — or note content — anywhere we can read it.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet** — solely so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch ads.
  Disabling Wi-Fi/data simply means no ads load; the app still works fully
  offline.

The app does **not** hold standing access to your location, contacts,
microphone, photos, or files, and it sends no notifications. When you
attach a file or restore a backup, the app uses your device's own file
picker and only touches the files you choose; attachments are stored as
copies in the app's private storage.

## Children

List Notes is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed for use
under the COPPA framework. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your choices

- **Disable personalized ads** — Android system settings → Privacy → Ads
- **Export your notes** — Settings → Back up notes (writes a JSON file you
  control)
- **Delete your data** — uninstall the app
- **Contact us** — dash.fusion@outlook.com
