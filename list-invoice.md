# List Invoice — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-26
**Last updated:** 2026-08-26

## Summary

List Invoice is an offline-first invoice maker. We do **not** create accounts,
do **not** ask for your email, and do **not** ship any analytics or tracking
SDK. Your business details, your clients, your line items and your amounts
never leave your device. The only data that leaves your device is what Google
AdMob needs to serve ads.

If that's all you wanted to know, you're done. The sections below spell it
out in detail.

## Who we are

- **App name:** List Invoice
- **Developer:** Dash Fusion
- **Support email:** dash.fusion@outlook.com
- **App listing:** Google Play (`com.dashfusion.listinvoice`)

## Data the app stores on your device

The following data is created and kept **locally** on your device. It never
leaves the device unless you explicitly export it or share a document.

- Your business profile: name, address, contact details, tax ID, and the logo
  image you choose
- Your saved clients, and your saved items with their usual rates
- Your invoices: line items, quantities, rates, discounts, tax, notes and
  payment terms, dates, statuses and totals
- Your invoice numbering settings, including the prefix, padding and the
  current counter
- App preferences such as theme and paper size

You can export all of it as a JSON backup file at any time via
**Settings → Export a backup**; the file is written where you choose and is
entirely under your control. You can delete invoices and clients inside the
app, wipe everything with **Settings → Delete all data**, or uninstall the
app — Android removes the local database with it.

The app explicitly opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device
transfer, so your invoices are not copied into Google's backup service.

We have no servers and no cloud storage. We cannot see your invoices and we
cannot recover your data if you lose your device.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK. It shows a full-screen
interstitial ad after a document is finished — a PDF exported or shared, or an
invoice marked paid — with frequency caps, and never while you are writing an
invoice. There is also an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch to
remove ads for 24 hours; nothing in the app is locked behind it. There are no
banner ads. Google may collect and process:

- Your device's **Advertising ID** (a resettable identifier that Android
  provides for ads)
- **Coarse location** (typically inferred from IP) and **device/network
  information** that AdMob needs to serve and measure ads
- **Ad interaction events** (an ad was requested, shown or clicked)
- **Crash logs and diagnostics**

These are used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention and security,
and are transmitted encrypted. AdMob does **not** receive your business
profile, your clients, your invoices or your amounts. Google's handling of the
data above is governed by Google's own policies:

- [AdMob privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

**Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** the app shows Google's certified
consent form before any ad is requested, and you choose whether personalised
ads are allowed. You can change your answer at any time from
**Settings → Ad privacy options** inside the app. You can also opt out of
personalised advertising via your device's system settings
(Android: **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We don't run any backend. We have no analytics SDK, no crash
reporter that phones home, no Firebase, and no custom telemetry. We don't log
app usage anywhere we can read it. The invoices you write and the clients you
save are yours alone.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet / network state** (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`) — solely
  so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch ads. Disabling Wi-Fi/data simply
  means no ads load; creating invoices, generating and sharing PDFs, printing,
  export and import all still work in airplane mode.
- **Advertising ID** (`AD_ID`) — required by Google Play for apps that serve
  ads; used by AdMob as described above.

The app requests **no storage permission**. Your logo is chosen through
Android's own system photo picker, so the app only ever sees the single image
you pick. Generated PDFs and backup files are written inside the app's own
storage and handed to other apps only when you tap share or print. The app
does **not** hold access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or
files.

## Children

List Invoice is intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to
children under 13 and is not designed for use under the COPPA framework. We do
not knowingly collect data from children.

## Your choices

- **Ad consent** — Settings → Ad privacy options (EEA/UK/Switzerland), or
  Android system settings → Privacy → Ads
- **Ad-free period** — watch one optional rewarded video for 24 hours without
  ads. Nothing else in the app is behind an ad: no watermark, no paper-size
  lock, no export limit, and backup is free
- **Export your data** — Settings → Export a backup (writes a JSON file you
  control)
- **Delete your data** — delete invoices or clients inside the app, use
  Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app
- **Contact us** — dash.fusion@outlook.com
