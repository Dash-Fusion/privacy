# Last Pack — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-15
**Last updated:** 2026-09-15

## Summary

Last Pack is a log book for stopping smoking. You record your quit plan, the
cravings you get through and any cigarette you smoke, and it adds them up: how
long since your last one, how many you have not smoked, and the money you have
not spent.

**Nothing you record ever leaves your phone.** There is no account, no sign-up,
no cloud, and no server of ours anywhere. We cannot see your quit date, your
cravings, the cigarettes you logged or your notes, and we could not hand them to
anyone if we were asked, because we never receive them.

It is **not a medical device**. It makes no claim about your body and gives no
medical advice. It records what you enter and does the arithmetic.

The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob needs to show ads,
and **nothing you logged is part of that**.

## Your record stays on your device

All of the following is created and kept **locally**, in a database only this
app can read, and never leaves your device unless you export it yourself:

- **Your quit plan:** the date and time you stopped (or plan to stop), how many
  cigarettes a day you smoked, how many come in a pack, and what a pack costs, in
  the currency you chose
- **Cravings:** when each one happened, how strong it was, what set it off if you
  chose to say, and any note you typed
- **Cigarettes you logged:** when, how many, and any note you typed
- **App preferences:** theme, whether to use your wallpaper's colours, when the
  app was first opened, when the rewarded ad-free window runs out, and a count
  the app uses to space out ads

We hold **no copy of any of it**. There is no analytics SDK in this app, no crash
reporter of our own, and no telemetry of any kind. Nothing you log is transmitted
anywhere, and none of it is used for advertising.

The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your
record is not copied into Google's backup service either.

**Consequence, stated honestly:** because the data exists only on your phone, we
cannot recover it if you lose the device or uninstall the app. Use **Settings →
Export a backup** and keep the file somewhere safe. The export is a plain,
readable JSON file, and **Settings → Restore a backup** reads it back on a new
phone.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app is supported by ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are the only reason the
app needs an internet connection at all; every logging, history and milestone
feature works fully offline.

The app shows **no banner ads**, and **never shows an ad while you are logging**
— not on Today, not while you log a craving or a cigarette, and not on your quit
plan. What it shows is an occasional full-screen ad when you close a past day's
page or the Milestones screen, and an optional rewarded ad you can choose to
watch to switch ads off for 24 hours.

To serve ads, Google receives data from your device, which may include your
device's advertising identifier (`AD_ID`), coarse location inferred from your IP
address, device and app information, and ad-interaction data. **It does not
receive your quit date, your cravings, the cigarettes you logged, your notes or
your money figures** — the app never sends those anywhere, and no ad targeting
here is based on them. Google's processing is done as an independent controller
under its own policies:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses information from partner sites and apps:
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>

### Consent (UMP)

Where required by law, the app shows Google's User Messaging Platform consent
form before ads are initialised, and records your choice. In the EEA and the UK
you are asked about personalised advertising; in covered US states you are
offered the opt-out required there. You can change your decision at any time
from **Settings → Ad privacy options**, and if you decline, every logging,
history and milestone feature continues to work exactly as before.

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We operate no servers, no accounts and no analytics. We have no
database with your name in it, and no way to identify you or your device. We do
not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet / network state** — solely so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch
  ads. Turning off Wi-Fi and mobile data means no ads load; every other feature
  still works.
- **Advertising ID (`AD_ID`)** — required by Google Play for apps that serve ads;
  used by AdMob as described above.

That is all. The app sends **no notifications and no reminders**, and it
requests no notification permission, no camera, no photos or storage, no
contacts, no location, no microphone, and no health-platform access. It does not
read or write Health Connect.

## Children

This app is for people who are stopping smoking. It is not directed at children
and is not designed for use by a child. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone — as described above, we hold no personal data at all.

## Your choices

- **Ad personalisation:** change your consent decision any time via
  **Settings → Ad privacy options**.
- **Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads** in Android's own
  settings, under **Settings → Google → Ads**.
- **Export your data** any time via **Settings → Export a backup**.
- **Delete your data** by deleting entries in the app, by **Settings → Start
  over**, which deletes the plan and everything logged, or by clearing the app's
  storage or uninstalling it. Because the data lives only on your device, that
  removes it completely and irreversibly — there is no copy anywhere else, and no
  deletion request to send us.
