# Neon PingPong — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-14
**Last updated:** 2026-08-14

## Summary

Neon PingPong is an offline-first arcade game. We do **not** create accounts,
do **not** ask for your email, and do **not** ship any analytics or tracking
SDK of our own. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob
needs to serve ads — and, if you play a Wi-Fi match, a tiny bit of game data
exchanged **directly with the other player's phone on your own network**.

If that's all you wanted to know, you're done. The sections below spell it
out in detail.

## Who we are

- **App name:** Neon PingPong
- **Developer:** Dash Fusion
- **Support email:** dash.fusion@outlook.com
- **App listing:** Google Play (`com.dashfusion.pingpong`)

## Data the app stores on your device

The following data is created and kept **locally** on your device. It never
leaves the device.

- App preferences: sound, music and vibration toggles, AI difficulty
- The expiry time of an ad-free period you earn from a rewarded ad

That's the whole list — the game keeps no profile, no match history and no
scores between sessions. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

We have no servers and no cloud storage.

## Wi-Fi multiplayer (local network only)

The optional **Wi-Fi Match** mode connects two phones **directly to each
other** over your local network. While hosting or joining:

- Your phone announces the game on the local network so nearby players can
  find it. That announcement includes your **device's model name** (for
  example "NeonPong · Pixel 9") so the other player knows what they're
  joining.
- During a match, ball and paddle positions and scores travel between the
  two phones.

All of this stays **inside your Wi-Fi network**, phone to phone. Nothing
about a multiplayer match is sent to us or to any server on the internet,
and nothing is stored after the match ends. If you never use Wi-Fi Match,
none of this happens at all.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect and process:

- Your device's **Advertising ID** (a resettable identifier that Android
  provides for ads)
- **Coarse location** (typically inferred from IP) and **device/network
  information** that AdMob needs to serve and measure ads
- **Ad interaction events** (impressions, clicks)

AdMob does **not** receive your game preferences or anything about your
matches. Google's handling of the data above is governed by Google's own
policies:

- [AdMob privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

**Consent:** in regions where it is required (for example the EEA and UK),
the app shows Google's consent dialog before any ads load, and you can
change your choice later via **Ad privacy options** on the main menu.
Interstitial ads appear only occasionally between matches, and the rewarded
video is always opt-in — watching one turns ads off for 24 hours.

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We don't run any backend. We have no analytics SDK, no crash
reporter that phones home, no Firebase, and no custom telemetry. We don't
log app usage anywhere we can read it.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet** — so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch ads, and so Wi-Fi
  Match can talk to the other phone **on your local network**. In airplane
  mode the game still works fully offline (solo and one-screen duel).
- **Vibrate** — for the haptic-feedback toggle.

The app does **not** hold access to your location, contacts, camera,
microphone, or files.

## Children

Neon PingPong is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed for
use under the COPPA framework. We do not knowingly collect data from
children.

## Your choices

- **Disable personalized ads** — Android system settings → Privacy → Ads
- **Go ad-free for 24 h** — watch the optional rewarded video
- **Delete your data** — uninstall the app
- **Contact us** — dash.fusion@outlook.com
