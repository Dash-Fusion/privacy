# List Budget — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-01
**Last updated:** 2026-08-01

## Summary

List Budget is an offline-first budget & expense tracker. Where your money
goes is deeply personal data, and we built the app around that: everything
you enter stays **on your device**. We do **not** create accounts, do
**not** ask for your email, do **not** link to any bank, and do **not**
ship any analytics or tracking SDK. The only data that leaves your device
is what Google AdMob needs to serve ads — and AdMob never sees your
budgets or expenses.

If that's all you wanted to know, you're done. The sections below spell it
out in detail.

## Who we are

- **App name:** List Budget
- **Developer:** Dash Fusion
- **Support email:** dash.fusion@outlook.com
- **App listing:** Google Play (`com.dashfusion.listbudget`)

## Data the app stores on your device

The following data is created and kept **locally** on your device. It never
leaves the device unless you explicitly export or share it.

- The budgets you set: categories, monthly amounts, and per-month overrides
- The expenses you log: amounts, categories, dates, and any notes you type
- App preferences: currency, theme, reminder settings

You can export all of it at any time via **Settings → Export backup** (a
single JSON file shared to any app or storage you choose), and import it
again via **Settings → Restore backup**. Nothing is uploaded by us — the
file goes only where you send it.

You can wipe everything by uninstalling the app — Android removes the local
database with it. We also disable Android's automatic cloud backup for this
app, so your spending data is never silently copied to a cloud account.

We have no servers and no cloud storage. We cannot see, recover, or reset
your data — it is yours alone.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect and process:

- Your device's **Advertising ID** (a resettable identifier that Android
  provides for ads)
- **Coarse location** (typically inferred from IP) and **device/network
  information** that AdMob needs to serve and measure ads
- **Ad interaction events** (impressions, clicks)

AdMob does **not** receive your budgets, expenses, categories, or
preferences. In addition, we block predatory-lending ad categories (payday
/ "fast cash" style ads) in our AdMob configuration — we don't think people
budgeting carefully should be steered toward expensive credit. Google's
handling of the data above is governed by Google's own policies:

- [AdMob privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where consent is
required, the app shows Google's consent dialog before any ad is requested,
and you can change your choice at any time via **Settings → Ad privacy
options**. Elsewhere, you can opt out of personalized advertising via your
device's system settings (Android: **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We don't run any backend. We have no analytics SDK, no crash
reporter that phones home, no Firebase, and no custom telemetry. We don't
log app usage anywhere we can read it.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet** — solely so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch ads.
  Disabling Wi-Fi/data simply means no ads load; the app still works fully
  offline.
- **Notifications** (Android 13+, optional) — asked only when you enable
  the daily logging reminder, so the app can show it. The reminder is
  scheduled locally on your device and contains no amounts or category
  names.

The app does **not** hold standing access to your location, contacts,
microphone, photos, or files. When you back up or restore, the app uses
your device's own file picker/share sheet and only touches the files you
choose.

## Children

List Budget is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed for
use under the COPPA framework. We do not knowingly collect data from
children.

## Your choices

- **Manage ad consent** — Settings → Ad privacy options (where applicable),
  or Android system settings → Privacy → Ads
- **Export your data** — Settings → Export backup (writes a backup file you
  control)
- **Delete your data** — uninstall the app
- **Contact us** — dash.fusion@outlook.com
