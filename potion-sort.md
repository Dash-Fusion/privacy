# Potion Sort — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-07
**Last updated:** 2026-08-07

## Summary

Potion Sort is an offline-first puzzle game. All 999 levels ship inside the
app and your progress lives **on your device**. We do **not** create
accounts and do **not** ask for your email. The only data that leaves your
device is what Google's ad and diagnostics services need to work — and none
of it identifies you personally.

If that's all you wanted to know, you're done. The sections below spell it
out in detail.

## Who we are

- **App name:** Potion Sort
- **Developer:** Dash Fusion
- **Support email:** dash.fusion@outlook.com
- **App listing:** Google Play (`com.dashfusion.potion_sort`)

## Data the app stores on your device

The following data is created and kept **locally** on your device:

- Your game progress: unlocked levels, stars, coins, and daily-challenge
  streaks
- App preferences: sound, haptics, and the color-blind symbols setting

There is no cloud save and no account. Uninstalling the app deletes this
data; we cannot see, recover, or reset it.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The game contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK. It shows an occasional
full-screen ad after you complete a level — never during play — and
optional rewarded ads that **you** choose to watch in exchange for in-game
boosters (extra undos, an extra vial, streak repair). Google may collect
and process:

- Your device's **Advertising ID** (a resettable identifier that Android
  provides for ads)
- **Coarse location** (typically inferred from IP) and **device/network
  information** that AdMob needs to serve and measure ads
- **Ad interaction events** (impressions, clicks, rewarded completions)

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own policies:

- [AdMob privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), the app shows a consent
dialog before serving personalized ads, and you may decline. You can also
opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device's system
settings (Android: **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

### Google Firebase (crash reporting & analytics)

To find crashes and fix unfair difficulty spikes, the app uses Firebase
Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics, which collect:

- **Crash logs** (device model, OS version, stack traces)
- **Anonymous gameplay events** — for example "level 42 completed in 30
  moves" or "rewarded ad watched"

These events contain no personal information, no account identifiers, and
nothing you typed. Firebase data is processed by Google under its own
terms: [Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We run no servers and no backend of our own. Everything above is
processed by Google's SDKs; we only ever see aggregated, anonymous
statistics.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet** — so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch ads and Firebase
  can upload crash reports. With no connection the game still works fully —
  ads simply don't appear.
- **Network state** — lets the ads SDK check whether a connection exists.

The app does **not** request access to your location, contacts, camera,
microphone, photos, or files, and it sends no notifications.

## Children

Potion Sort is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly
collect data from children.

## Your choices

- **Disable personalized ads** — Android system settings → Privacy → Ads
- **Play without ads appearing** — the game is fully playable offline
- **Delete your data** — uninstall the app
- **Contact us** — dash.fusion@outlook.com
