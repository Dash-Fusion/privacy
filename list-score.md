# List Score — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-06-11
**Last updated:** 2026-06-11

## Summary

List Score is an offline-first score keeper. We do **not** create accounts,
do **not** ask for your email, and do **not** ship any analytics or tracking
SDK. The only data that leaves your device is what Google AdMob needs to
serve ads.

If that's all you wanted to know, you're done. The sections below spell it
out in detail.

## Who we are

- **App name:** List Score
- **Developer:** Dash Fusion
- **Support email:** dash.fusion@outlook.com
- **App listing:** Google Play (`com.dashfusion.listscore`)

## Data the app stores on your device

The following data is created and kept **locally** on your device. It never
leaves the device unless you explicitly export it.

- Games you create: name, scoring mode, win condition, target score
- Players: names, colors, and your saved player library
- Every score entry and round, including the full edit history
- App preferences: theme (system/light/dark) and the keep-screen-awake
  toggle

You can export all of this as a JSON file at any time via
**Settings → Export backup** — the file goes only where you choose to share
it. You can wipe everything by deleting games in the app or by uninstalling
it — Android removes the local database with it.

We have no servers and no cloud storage. We cannot recover your data if you
lose your device.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect and process:

- Your device's **Advertising ID** (a resettable identifier that Android
  provides for ads)
- **Coarse location** (typically inferred from IP) and **device/network
  information** that AdMob needs to serve and measure ads
- **Ad interaction events** (impressions, clicks)

AdMob does **not** receive your games, players, scores, or preferences.
Google's handling of the data above is governed by Google's own policies:

- [AdMob privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland the app
shows a consent message before serving ads, as required by Google's EU User
Consent Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device's
system settings (Android: **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We don't run any backend. We have no analytics SDK, no crash
reporter that phones home, no Firebase, and no custom telemetry. We don't
log app usage anywhere we can read it.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet** — solely so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch ads.
  Disabling Wi-Fi/data simply means no ads load; the app still works fully
  offline.
- **Wake lock** — for the optional keep-screen-awake toggle during long
  game nights.

The app does **not** request location, contacts, photos, microphone,
camera, notifications, or any other sensitive permission.

## Children

List Score is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed for
use under the COPPA framework. We do not knowingly collect data from
children.

## Your choices

- **Disable personalized ads** — Android system settings → Privacy → Ads
- **Export your data** — Settings → Export backup (writes a JSON file you
  control)
- **Delete your data** — delete games in the app, or uninstall it
- **Contact us** — dash.fusion@outlook.com

If we change the data we collect or the third-party services we integrate,
we will update this page and bump the "Last updated" date above.
