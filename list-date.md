# List Date — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-06-19
**Last updated:** 2026-06-19

## Summary

List Date is an offline-first date calculator (days between dates, add/subtract
durations, age, working days, week number) that can save labelled results. We
do **not** create accounts, do **not** ask for your email, and do **not** ship
any analytics or tracking SDK. The only data that leaves your device is what
Google AdMob needs to serve ads.

If that's all you wanted to know, you're done. The sections below spell it
out in detail.

## Who we are

- **App name:** List Date
- **Developer:** Dash Fusion
- **Support email:** dash.fusion@outlook.com
- **App listing:** Google Play (`com.dashfusion.listdate`)

## Data the app stores on your device

The following data is created and kept **locally** on your device. It never
leaves the device unless you explicitly export it.

- Your saved results and their labels — the dates you pick and the names you
  type in (e.g. "Days until exam", "Baby's age")
- The inputs behind each saved result (start/end dates, durations, birth date,
  and any custom holiday dates), so ages and countdowns can recompute to today
  each time you open the app
- App preferences: theme (system/light/dark)

You can export this data as a JSON file at any time via
**Settings → Back up saved results**, and import it again via
**Settings → Restore from backup**. You can wipe all of it by uninstalling
the app — Android removes the local database with it.

We have no servers and no cloud storage. We cannot recover your data if you
lose your device.

## Third-party services we use

### Google AdMob (ads)

The app contains the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect and process:

- Your device's **Advertising ID** (a resettable identifier that Android
  provides for ads)
- **Coarse location** (typically inferred from IP) and **device/network
  information** that AdMob needs to serve and measure ads
- **Ad interaction events** (impressions, clicks)

AdMob does **not** receive your saved results, your dates, or your
preferences. Google's handling of the data above is governed by Google's
own policies:

- [AdMob privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device's
system settings (Android: **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

## Data we collect ourselves

**None.** We don't run any backend. We have no analytics SDK, no crash
reporter that phones home, no Firebase, and no custom telemetry. We don't
log app usage anywhere we can read it.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Internet** — solely so the Google Mobile Ads SDK can fetch ads.
  Disabling Wi-Fi/data simply means no ads load; the app still works fully
  offline.

The app does **not** hold standing access to your location, contacts,
microphone, photos, or files. When you back up or restore, the app uses your
device's own share sheet / file picker and only touches the single file you
choose.

## Children

List Date is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed for
use under the COPPA framework. We do not knowingly collect data from
children.

## Your choices

- **Disable personalized ads** — Android system settings → Privacy → Ads
- **Export your data** — Settings → Back up saved results (writes a JSON file
  you control)
- **Delete your data** — uninstall the app
- **Contact us** — dash.fusion@outlook.com
